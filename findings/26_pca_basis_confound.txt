FINDING 26 — THE NODE FEATURES DO CARRY SIGNAL; FINDING 06 WAS A BASIS ARTEFACT
================================================================================
Status: measured, decisive, SUPERSEDES FINDING 06
Date:   2026-09-01
Raw:    results/pca_basis_test.json
Code:   scripts/run_pca_basis_test.py
Origin: reviewer question 1 (per-subject PCA alignment)


WHAT FINDING 06 CLAIMED
-----------------------
"The per-region features contain no diagnostic information. Not compressed
information, not information the graph fails to exploit -- none."

  quantum latents, all 200 regions        AUC 0.459
  correlation profile (19,900-d)          AUC 0.693

That conclusion drove the whole root-cause narrative of the ABIDE graph study:
the graph sits at chance because its inputs are empty.


WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG
-----------------------
compress_connectivity_profiles() fits a separate PCA for every subject. Region
i's feature vector is its connectivity profile projected onto THAT SUBJECT'S
principal axes. Component 3 means something different for every subject, so a
classifier comparing component 3 across subjects is comparing incommensurable
quantities. The signal was never absent; it was rotated into a different frame
per subject.

Nothing about this is specific to the quantum arm. It affects every model that
consumes per-region node features, including the classical GCN baselines, which
is why they sit at chance too.


THE TEST
--------
Identical leave-site-out folds, 1035 subjects, 20 sites, linear SVM on
flattened node features. Three feature constructions:

  per-subject   PCA fitted on each subject's own profiles (what we used)
  fold-wise     one PCA basis fitted on TRAINING-SITE profiles only, applied
                unchanged to the held-out site
  ceiling       flattened correlation upper triangle, 19,900-d, no compression

  arm            mean AUC        folds > 0.60
  ------------   -------------   ------------
  per-subject    0.541 +- 0.070      6 / 20
  fold-wise      0.739 +- 0.095     19 / 20
  ceiling        0.733 +- 0.090     18 / 20

  fold-wise vs per-subject   +0.198 AUC   p = 9.5e-06   wins 18/20 sites
  fold-wise vs ceiling       +0.006 AUC   p = 0.93      wins 10/20 sites


WHAT THIS MEANS
---------------
1. FINDING 06 IS FALSIFIED. Per-region node features carry the diagnostic
   signal. Sixteen components per region in a shared basis are statistically
   indistinguishable from the full 19,900-dimensional correlation vector
   (p = 0.93), so the compression loses nothing that matters.

2. THE ABIDE GRAPH RESULTS ARE CONFOUNDED. Every conclusion in Section V that
   rests on "the graph pipeline sits at chance because its node features are
   empty" is unsupported. The features were fine; the basis was not. The graph
   experiments must be re-run with fold-wise features before anything can be
   concluded about the graph architecture.

3. THE SCOPE IS LIMITED TO THE GRAPH STUDY. The model suite (QCNN, VQC, QSVMs,
   TQEK and their matched classical arms), Plans A/B/C, the low-data test and
   the calibration test all consume build_descriptors() output -- graph-theoretic
   summaries or the raw correlation vector -- with feature selection and scaling
   fitted on training sites only. None of them touches
   compress_connectivity_profiles. The 284 matched comparisons, the QCNN result,
   the kernel tie and the overfitting result are unaffected.

4. THE FIDELITY CONCENTRATION RESULT IS UNAFFECTED. It is measured on the
   quantum states themselves, not on downstream accuracy, and the collapse at
   16 qubits is a property of the encoding independent of what the features
   mean.

5. THE INVARIANCE DERIVATION IS UNAFFECTED. It is algebraic.


WHY IT WAS MISSED
-----------------
The docstring of compress_region_timeseries says the PCA yields "a compact
temporal signature in a shared component basis", and that is true -- the basis
is shared across REGIONS within a subject, which is what the fidelity kernel
needs, since it only ever compares regions of the same subject. It is not
shared across subjects, which is what the downstream classifier needs. Both
statements are correct and only one of them was checked.

FINDING 06 tested the features directly and found chance, which looked like
confirmation. It could not distinguish "no signal" from "signal in an
incomparable frame", and we read it as the former.


WHAT TO DO NEXT
---------------
- Re-run run_abide_study.py with a fold-wise basis fitted inside each LSO fold.
- Revise Section V: the root-cause claim, the topology ablation reading, and
  the classification-performance narrative all depend on FINDING 06.
- Keep Sections VI-IX as they stand.

  Proposed (quantum + GAT)     0.504 +- 0.028  0.196 +- 0.135      20 / 20

TWO THINGS FOLLOW, AND THEY POINT IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS.

1. The reviewer's confound is real and PART 1 stands. The node features carry
   the signal once the basis is shared, and FINDING 06's root-cause claim is
   retracted.

2. Correcting it does not move the graph pipeline. Given features a linear SVM
   reads at AUC 0.739, the GAT returns 0.504 and the GCN 0.512, both at the
   majority rate. The failure is downstream of the node features.

THE GRAPH RESULT IS NOT A MEASUREMENT.
Every fold, for both models, is a degenerate single-class predictor:
specificity is exactly 1.000 or exactly 0.000, twenty times out of twenty. The
model predicts one class for every subject in the held-out site and its
accuracy is the majority rate by construction. This is precisely what the
degeneracy screen of FINDING 24 was built to reject, and it rejects this.

IT IS NOT A TRAINING-LENGTH ARTEFACT.
MAD collapsing toward zero suggested over-smoothing, which would be tunable.
Holding features and folds fixed and varying only epochs:

  site      3 epochs              10 epochs             30 epochs
  -------   -------------------   -------------------   -------------------
  OHSU      acc 0.538  F1 0.000   acc 0.462  F1 0.632   acc 0.462  F1 0.632
  OLIN      acc 0.441  F1 0.000   acc 0.441  F1 0.000   acc 0.559  F1 0.717
  PITT      acc 0.518  F1 0.682   acc 0.482  F1 0.000   acc 0.518  F1 0.682

Degenerate at every epoch count, flipping between all-positive and
all-negative without ever discriminating. More training does not help and less
training does not either.

WHAT CAN HONESTLY BE CLAIMED
  - The node features carry the diagnostic signal (PART 1, measured).
  - The graph pipeline as implemented cannot use them (PART 2, measured).
  - WHY it cannot is open. With 20/20 folds degenerate we can say the
    classifier fails to train on this graph, and we cannot attribute that to
    the topology, the propagation or the read-out without further work.

WHAT WAS NOT RUN, AND WHY
A 16-qubit version was started for like-for-like comparison with the original
run and stopped at 14/20 folds. At that width fidelity is degenerate by
FINDING 01, so the topology carries nothing regardless of feature quality and
the run could only reproduce "graph at chance" for a second, already-documented
reason. Its partial output agrees with the 8-qubit run: accuracy 0.515, 14/14
folds one-class.
