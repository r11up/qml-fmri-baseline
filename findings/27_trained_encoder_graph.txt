FINDING 27 — TRAINING THE ENCODER DOES NOT RESCUE THE GRAPH PIPELINE
=====================================================================
Status: measured, 20 of 20 ABIDE I site folds
Date:   2026-09-19
Code:   scripts/run_graph_trained_encoder.py
Output: results/graph_trained_encoder_q8.json


WHY
---
The reported graph runs (run_abide_study.py, run_graph_foldwise.py) evaluate
the encoder once, under torch.no_grad(), at its initial parameters (every
encoding gain = 1), select the top-k support from that single encoding, and
cache both. Only the GAT/GCN and the edge kernel (alpha, beta, pair MLP) are
optimised. The manuscript described the encoder as trained, so the negative
graph result did not yet cover the architecture as described.


WHAT WAS RUN
------------
Settings: ABIDE I, 8 qubits, fold-wise PCA basis, mixed seed kernel, k = 20,
30 epochs, Adam lr 0.01 with weight decay 5e-4, seed 0. The settings match
results/graph_foldwise_q8.json.

Two arms per fold go through identical code, folds and seeds:

  frozen   encoder fixed at initialisation, support selected once
           (the reported configuration)
  trained  encoder gains and angles in the optimiser, receiving gradients
           through the Pauli-Z latents; top-k support re-selected from the
           current encoder at the start of every epoch; held-out support
           selected with the final encoder

Sanity check: the frozen arm reproduces graph_foldwise_q8.json exactly on
20/20 folds for the GCN and 19/20 for the GAT.

What moved:

What did not move:
  training accuracy stays at the share of controls in the training set,
  so the classifier never fits even its training data


INTERPRETATION
--------------
Training the encoder changes the graph substantially and the outcome not at
all. The collapse to a single predicted class survives four conditions:

  good node features (FINDING 26)
  repaired topology
  four read-outs
  a jointly trained encoder with a re-selected support

The cause lies after the encoder, in the classifier.

Caveat on "the encoder learned": under Adam, weight decay moves parameters
even when their loss gradient is near zero. The GCN's gains drifted toward
zero (final range 0.00-0.61). Describe the run as "the encoder was trained
and the support re-selected", not as "the encoder learned features".
